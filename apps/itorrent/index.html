01 About

iTorrent is a fully featured open-source torrent client for iOS. It keeps downloading in the background, integrates with the Files app, supports magnet links, sequential download and per-file selection, and ships a built-in VLC-based player with Picture in Picture, audio and subtitle tracks and playback speed control.

Release notes
iTorrent 2.2.0 | iTorrent

## What's new:
* Meet the new built-in VLC player. Play compatible video and audio files directly in iTorrent, even while they are still downloading.
* Enjoy Picture in Picture, playback speeds from 0.5x to 2x, audio and subtitle track selection, precise seeking, and system media controls.
* Take playback to a bigger screen with AirPlay or a compatible player discovered on your local network.
* Choose how pieces are downloaded with Sequential and First & Last priority modes, making it easier to start watching sooner.
* Select and share multiple completed files or entire folders in one go.

## Improvements:
* Refreshed the interface for iOS 26, with Liquid Glass styling, cleaner dialogs, and more polished navigation.
* Made torrent details smoother and easier to use, with clearer download-priority controls and private-torrent information.
* Improved media previewing so iTorrent can offer the best available player for each file.
* Updated the torrent and media engines for better compatibility, stability, and recovery from unexpected errors.
* Expanded and refined translations across all supported languages.

## Fixes:
* Fixed cases where magnet links could be saved incorrectly.
* Fixed a startup race condition that could interfere with launching the app or completing initial setup.
* Fixed toolbars and navigation behaving incorrectly in iPad split-screen mode.
* Fixed RSS downloads returning to the wrong screen.
* Fixed torrent details sometimes getting stuck in a continuous update loop.
* Fixed several playback, AirPlay, and local-device connection issues.

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/XITRIX/iTorrent/compare/v2.1.1-1...v2.2.0-1
