01 About

TrollInstallerX installs TrollStore on compatible iOS devices - arm64 (iOS 14.0 - 16.6.1) and arm64e (iOS 14.0 - 16.5.1) - using the DirtyCow exploit.

Upstream publishes no screenshots for this app, and OmniSource does not invent them.

TrollInstallerX 1.0.3 | TrollInstallerX

# TrollInstallerX v1.0.3

## Changes:
* Update for Xcode 15.4+ compilation (fixes #25)
* Added a temporary fix for yellow iPhone 14 (Plus) devices on iOS 16.3, please use `TrollInstallerX-20D50.ipa` (fixes #33)
* Removing confusing logging around exploit deinitialisation (e.g. #11)
* Fix arm64e devices on iOS 15.7.x that couldn't download the kernelcache by updating libgrabkernel2 (should, in theory, fix #21)

**Note**: A8 devices on iOS 15.2+ will not be supported until a future release of TrollInstallerX. Furthermore, support for arm64e (A12+) devices on early iOS 17.0 betas is not planned, unless someone else would like to implement it. You can now use [TrollRestore](https://github.com/JJTech0130/TrollRestore) for both of these cases.

# DO NOT DOWNLOAD THE 20D50 IPA UNLESS YOU HAVE A YELLOW IPHONE 14 (PLUS) ON IOS 16.3.
