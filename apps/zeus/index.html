01 About

Zeus is a free and open-source, self-custodial Bitcoin and Lightning wallet for iPhone and iPad. Connect the app to your own node (LND, Core Lightning, c-lightning-REST, BTCPay Server or Lightning Terminal) or use the built-in Olympus LNDHub for a quick start, with contacts, Nostr Wallet Connect, LNURL, point-of-sale and full on-chain and Lightning payment support.
