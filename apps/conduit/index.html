01 About

Conduit is a native, open-source iOS and Android client for Open WebUI, direct OpenAI-compatible endpoints, Ollama and Hermes. Chat with your own self-hosted local models or API-backed ones with a polished SwiftUI interface — conversations stay in sync, code blocks render properly, and your data never touches a third-party server.

Upstream publishes no screenshots for this app, and OmniSource does not invent them.
