01 About

SpotiFLAC Mobile is an open-source Spotify track and playlist metadata downloader and FLAC converter for iOS and Android. Search and download high-quality FLAC and MP3 audio with complete ID3 metadata, embedded album artwork, and synchronized lyrics directly on your device.
