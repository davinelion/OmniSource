Good to know

Re-signing cadence. A free Apple Account re-signs every 7 days and holds two apps beside the client itself; a paid developer account extends that to 90 days. AltStore and SideStore refresh for you — AltStore over Wi-Fi via its desktop companion, SideStore on-device through its local VPN. Feather and ESign sign on demand, so re-signing an app that ran out of days is a manual tap. Apps installed inside LiveContainer ride on its signature instead of using a slot of their own.

Shared bundle IDs. YouTube-family apps (YTLite, YouPro, uYouEnhanced…) share one bundle identifier — installing a new one replaces the previous. The catalog flags these conflicts.

Updates. Every six hours the pipeline re-syncs from official upstreams, re-probes all downloads and publishes new feeds. Your client picks up new versions the next time it checks the source.

Trust. Each entry shows its verification level, checksum (when published by upstream) and exact provenance. Broken links are reported automatically to a tracking issue.
